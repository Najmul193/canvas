Social Media Executive

--CANVAS--

Apply Now!

As a Social Media Executive at Canvas, you will be at the heart of our brand storytelling—crafting digital content that connects our heritage-inspired fashion and jewelry collections to the world. You will work closely with our creative and marketing teams to design campaigns, create visually compelling content, manage social media channels, and monitor engagement analytics.

We are seeking a highly creative graphic designer with a strategic mindset who can translate our brand values into digital formats. If you love heritage, storytelling, design, and making an impact through social platforms, we want you on our team.

 

Creativity & Autonomy
Visual Design
Technical Expertise
Administrative Work
Marketing Analysis

Responsibilities

  • Manage Canvas’s presence across all social media platforms (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, etc.)
  • Design engaging posts, stories, reels, and content using Photoshop/Illustrator
  • Plan and execute social media marketing campaigns
  • Maintain a consistent visual identity and brand tone
  • Create monthly performance reports and analyze engagement metrics
  • Collaborate with the founder/creative director for campaign storytelling
  • Monitor customer trends and market (competitor) activities 

Must Have

  • Degree or diploma in Graphic Design, Marketing, or related field (Preferred - Not mandatory)
  • Professional experience with Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and Canva
  • Proven experience in managing brand social media pages
  • Strong copywriting and content planning skills
  • Understanding of marketing funnels, reach, and engagement strategy
  • Fluent in English and Bengali 
  • Excellent time management and multitasking ability

Nice to have

  • Experience with short-form video editing (CapCut, Adobe Premiere, etc.)
  • Familiarity with paid ad platforms (Meta Ads Manager)
  • Google Adwords experience
  • Interest in heritage crafts or fashion
  • Basic photography or animation skills

What's great about the job?

  • Be part of a team that celebrates heritage, creativity, and innovation
  • Friendly, open work culture—where your ideas are valued
  • No micromanagement, flexible working hours
  • Creative autonomy with clear responsibilities
  • Hands-on experience in storytelling, design, and digital campaigns
  • Opportunities to work on exhibitions, product launches, and collaborations
  • Direct impact on shaping Canvas's brand voice and identity
  • Freedom to experiment, fail, learn, and grow
