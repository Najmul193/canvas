    When we think of inspiration in design, we often look at art, history, or culture. But what about nature itself—specifically, the Sundarbans?

    The Sundarbans isn’t just a forest; it’s Bangladesh’s first line of defense.

    This vast mangrove ecosystem absorbs the impact of floods and cyclones, saving millions of lives every year and sheltering some of the world's most endangered species. Despite its critical role in climate resilience, it remains underrated and often overlooked.

    Why not celebrate its significance through design?

    Our Research Board explores the essence of the Sundarbans—its biodiversity, textures, and organic forms. From the Royal Bengal Tiger to the intricate patterns of Kingfisher feathers, each element tells a story of survival, adaptability, and raw beauty.

    Elements that shaped this research:

    The Royal Bengal Tiger – Strength & elegance

    The Eagle & Kingfisher – Sharp, structured forms

    The Serpent & Vines – Fluid, organic jewelry structures

    The Crocodile Scales & Fish Textures – Surface detailing in accessories

    Design isn’t just about aesthetics—it’s about storytelling. It’s about carrying forward something meaningful and powerful, just like the Sundarbans itself. Every detail in our upcoming designs pays tribute to this breathtaking ecosystem, translating nature into wearable art.

    𝐀𝐝𝐨𝐫𝐧𝐞𝐝 𝐢𝐧 𝐇𝐞𝐫𝐢𝐭𝐚𝐠𝐞, 𝐒𝐭𝐲𝐥𝐞𝐝 𝐟𝐨𝐫 𝐏𝐮𝐫𝐩𝐨𝐬𝐞

    Inspired by the vibrant biodiversity and enduring spirit of the Sundarbans, this handcrafted jewelry and accessories collection celebrates today's empowered women—champions of heritage preservation. Bold yet graceful, each brass motif narrates a story of commitment, courage, and the sacred bond between womanhood and nature.

    Carry your heritage proudly, making every statement count.

    Our Sketch Board captures the spirit of the Sundarbans in wearable art.

    𝐒𝐮𝐧𝐠𝐥𝐚𝐬𝐬𝐞𝐬 – A bold statement piece featuring an eagle at the center, cranes, tigers, deer, and intricate forest details. The arms are uniquely crafted with a python motif, symbolizing strength and resilience.

    𝐍𝐞𝐜𝐤𝐩𝐢𝐞𝐜𝐞 – At its heart lies the Royal Bengal Tiger, Bangladesh’s national animal, set within a regal brass structure.

    𝐇𝐚𝐧𝐝𝐛𝐚𝐠 – Crafted with pure, eco-friendly leather and reusable brass, this design captures the essence of the Sundarbans, showcasing the tiger, deer, birds, river elements, and dried summer plants. The brass handle adds a luxurious yet sustainable touch.

    Every element in this collection is a tribute to the Sundarbans’ ecosystem, seamlessly blending heritage, craftsmanship, sustainability, and storytelling. We’re thrilled to bring this vision to life! By wearing these pieces, you become part of a powerful story - that celebrates nature, culture, and conservation while embracing sustainable luxury. Our commitment to the planet shines through every detail, from eco-friendly leather to brass that can be reshaped and repurposed, ensuring that beauty never comes at nature’s expense.
