Our Story

Decorated Handcrafted Jewelry Store

About Us

Canvas is an exclusive handcrafted jewelry boutique, specializing in offering exquisitely designed pieces forged from the finest brass and silver. Each item in our collection is the product of painstaking craftsmanship, marrying the timeless grace of classic styles with the clean lines of modern design, ideal for those who cherish sophistication with a modern twist. At Canvas, we believe that jewelry is a profound expression of personal style and individuality. As such, each piece is crafted with the utmost care and attention to detail, ensuring it not only complements but enhances your unique personal narrative.


Explore our extensive selection of artisanal jewelry, where each piece is not just stylish but thoughtfully created to reflect your distinct taste and personality. Canvas provides more than just adornments; we offer wearable art that resonates with your life and style. Whether it’s for a momentous celebration or just to elevate your daily attire, our creations are destined to become the cornerstone of your jewelry collection.

Our Vision

Our mission at Canvas is to redefine the art of jewelry making by infusing every piece with a sense of purpose and passion. We are committed to preserving the traditional techniques of handcraftsmanship while embracing innovative designs that set new trends in the jewelry industry. Our vision is to create a legacy of timeless pieces that enhance the beauty and confidence of those who wear them. We strive to be a brand that not only stands for quality and beauty but also supports sustainable practices and responsible sourcing to ensure that our creations are as ethical as they are elegant. At Canvas, we aim to inspire and be inspired by our customers, crafting jewelry that truly makes a difference in the world and in the lives of those who wear our pieces.

To create something exceptional, your mindset must be relentlessly focused on the smallest detail. 

-Giorgio Armani

Our Founder


Tanwy Kabir

Owner

Tanwy Kabir is the founder of Canvas, a distinguished handcrafted jewelry brand that merges traditional craftsmanship with contemporary aesthetics. Inspired by early exposure to local artisans, Tanwy launched Canvas to create jewelry that combines elegance with personal expression. Her designs are known for their precision and elegance, reflecting a belief that jewelry should resonate deeply with its wearer.


Committed to sustainability, Tanwy ensures that each piece is crafted using ethically sourced materials. As a leader in the jewelry design community, she is an advocate for the arts and continues to inspire with her commitment to creating meaningful and beautiful jewelry.
